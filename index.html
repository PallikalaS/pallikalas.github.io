The Company
CurriculumMind.

Every child deserves a tutor as patient and personalized as the best private tutor in Palo Alto, for the price of a Netflix subscription. CurriculumMind is an AI tutor for grade 6 through 12 that answers Socratically at the child's actual level, grounded in verified Common Core curriculum. Built on an agentic RAG platform with stateful tutoring workflows, academic integrity guardrails, and live mastery checks.

Socratic by design
Answers at the child's level. No walls of text. No condescension.
Common Core RAG
Grounded retrieval across curriculum. No hallucinated standards.
Homework guardrails
Detects uploaded assignments. Guides, never cheats.
Live mastery checks
Progress only when truly understood. Measured, not assumed.
